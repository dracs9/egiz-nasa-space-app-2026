# Sources and attribution inventory

Written with AI assistance (Codex); human review pending. Accessed 2026-10-15. This is a source inventory, not a submission approval or scientific-validation claim. Row-level keys and citations are in `data/curated/sources.csv`.

- **Lunar topography:** NASA GSFC [PGDA product 78](https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/78); Barker et al. (2021), *Improved LOLA Elevation Maps for South Pole Landing Sites: Error Estimates and Their Impact on Illumination Conditions*, [doi:10.1016/j.pss.2020.105119](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pss.2020.105119). The real pilot uses the linked Site20v2 5 m product, in MOON_ME/DE421, not synthetic NASA observations.
- **Mars topography:** USGS Astrogeology, NASA MGS/MOLA and ESA Mars Express/HRSC, [Mars MGS MOLA – MEX HRSC blended DEM 200 m v2 metadata](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/Mars/Topography/HRSC_MOLA_Blend/Mars_HRSC_MOLA_BlendShade_Global_200mp_v2.lbl.xml). Credit NASA/JPL/GSFC, ESA/DLR/FU Berlin and USGS as specified in the product. The pilot caches only a Jezero subset from the linked DEM.
- **Earth topography:** [Copernicus Digital Elevation Model GLO-30 Public, 2021 release, AWS Registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/). Copernicus DEM was accessed on 2026-10-15 from the registry. Before publication, review the [current Copernicus licence linked by the registry](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM) and retain its required provider attribution. The source is a DSM including surface features.
- **Solar calculation:** NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory [Solar Calculation Details](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html), based on Meeus; astropy for independent numerical geometry tests. The implementation reports its measured comparison and atmospheric assumptions.
- **Historical lunar candidate regions:** NASA (2024), [NASA Provides Update on Artemis III Moon Landing Regions](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-provides-update-on-artemis-iii-moon-landing-regions/). The pilot center is chosen from a PGDA raster extent and is not an official selected base location.
- **Jezero landing coordinates:** Bell et al. (2022), [Geological, multispectral, and meteorological imaging results from the Mars 2020 Perseverance rover in Jezero crater](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo4856). The manuscript reports the actual landing point, used as the analysis center.
- **Documented field practice:** NASA (2022), [NASA to Practice Artemis Moonwalking, Roving Operations in Arizona Desert](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasa-to-practice-artemis-moonwalking-roving-operations-in-arizona-desert/). JETT3 used artificial polar lighting. That case is excluded from validation of natural solar windows. A nearby coordinate is from the [Arizona Geological Survey S P Crater image record](https://azgs.arizona.edu/birds-eye-view-sp-crater-arizona), and is not claimed to be an exact JETT traverse point.
- **Physical-gap alternatives:** NASA [Parabolic Flight](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/parabolic-flight/), NASA SSRI [Thermal Vacuum](https://s3vi.ndc.nasa.gov/ssri-kb/topics/59/), and NASA [Analog Missions](https://www.nasa.gov/analog-missions/). These support separate test-method descriptions; the Earth rankings do not reproduce all spaceflight hazards.

The following catalog products are implemented as acquisition adapters but were **not acquired in this session**: [MOD11A2 v6.1](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MOD11A2), [MYD11A2 v6.1](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MYD11A2), [GPM IMERG monthly V07](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/NASA_GPM_L3_IMERG_MONTHLY_V07), [ESA WorldCover v200](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/ESA_WorldCover_v200), [MODIS MCD12Q1 v6.1](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MCD12Q1), [MODIS MOD10A1 v6.1](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MOD10A1), and [LSIB 2017](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/USDOS_LSIB_SIMPLE_2017). Actual versions, observation periods, licences and source provenance must be captured after the authenticated run. ESA WorldCover requires CC BY 4.0 attribution. NASA source names are retained; no NASA logo or endorsement is claimed.

Target temperatures remain blank; no generic planetary thermal values are presented as measurements at either analysis location. Mission-template scales and weights come from the project's provisional mathematical specification, not from a NASA approval.
