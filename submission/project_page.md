# Egiz: Earth test campaigns for Moon and Mars

## Summary

Egiz selects up to three complementary Earth test sites for a lunar base region or Martian landing site. It compares terrain at consistent metric baselines, scores mission objectives, and explains what Earth cannot reproduce. This local release demonstrates the software with clearly marked synthetic measurements. Real-data scientific recommendations require acquisition, preregistration, and independent review.

## How it works

Choose a target and the mission tests that matter. Campaign coverage combines the best fidelity for each objective across selected sites. Exact shortlist search and a full-pool greedy check produce a frontier for one, two, and three sites. Site details expose attributes, formulas, missing values, and monthly lighting inputs. Evidence presents all experiment statuses, including those that have not run.

Python handles raster processing, Earth Engine acquisition, NOAA solar positions, terrain shadows, and deterministic JSON. The static browser application also runs from a local offline package and contains no generative AI service.

## Data status and limits

Bundled campaign measurements are invented fixtures, not a completed NASA/ESA observational analysis. References distinguish planned datasets and verified acquisition. Fidelity does not establish suitability, permissions, logistics, safety, or physical equivalence. Gravity, atmosphere, radiation, and regolith require separate facilities. No field discovery or scientific validation is claimed.
