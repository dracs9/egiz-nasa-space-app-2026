# Destination artwork

These assets serve only as decorative interface artwork in Mission Brief and the campaign entry. They are not satellite observations, source DEMs, site evidence, or calculated simulation frames. Scientific terrain, NASA planetary textures, engineering schematics, recorded telemetry and numerical exports are unchanged.

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool, 20 October 2026. No external image references were supplied. No attribution badge is added to the interface. Original PNGs are retained in the workspace under `artifacts/artwork/`; production JPEGs and responsive versions are under `web/public/artwork/`.

Full images are 1536 × 1024; responsive versions are 768 × 512. JPEG conversion uses macOS `sips`, quality 90 for the full asset and 88 for the responsive asset. Components request the appropriate size using `srcset`; repeated instances share the browser cache. Images are decorative (`alt=""`, `aria-hidden="true"`), while target names and coordinates remain accessible text. The selected target controls the summary and campaign artwork.

## Final Moon prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium planetary engineering application destination card, wide landscape artwork, 1536 by 1024 composition. Primary request: an exceptionally detailed cinematic orbital view of Earth's Moon, with a large curved lunar globe filling the frame, near the cratered southern highlands, a rugged surface of overlapping impact craters and realistic charcoal grey basalt and pale silver regolith. Camera close enough to reveal intricate crater rims and soft ejecta, but clearly seeing the globe's curved limb. Deep black empty space in the upper third and at the left edge. Natural grazing sunlight from the upper right, beautiful crisp silver rim light, physically believable sharp airless shadows, restrained very subtle grain, sophisticated astronomical documentary cinematography. Subject is placed mostly on the right and lower frame, leave darker negative space toward bottom left for the app to add legible labels later. Photorealistic premium 3D space artwork, spectacular depth and clean composition, high dynamic range without artificial glow. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no border, no people, no spacecraft, no Earth in the sky, no lens flares, no atmosphere on the Moon. It is decorative destination artwork, not a scientific map; do not add cartographic labels.
