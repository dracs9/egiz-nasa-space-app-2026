**Subject: Astana 2026 — eligibility clarification for an existing October prototype and event arrangements**

Hello Space Apps Astana organizers,

We are preparing to participate in the 2026 challenge “Identify Earth Locations that Analog the Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars” with a project called Egiz. We have read the current Participant FAQ and Team Formation Guide, including the requirement not to begin challenge work before November 14 at 09:00 local time.

We need to disclose an existing situation accurately. Planning and methodology documents were prepared before the event. On October 15, at a repository user's request, AI-assisted local implementation was undertaken: a Python processing and campaign-selection engine, JSON contracts, a React application, synthetic fixtures, a small real-raster research pilot, tests/builds, and draft slides/submission text. Some files are uncommitted. These are challenge-specific materials, not merely a generic template or unrelated practice. The application distinguishes synthetic measurements from a small measured terrain pilot; scientific validation is incomplete. Nothing has been submitted for judging. Development was paused after rechecking the current rule, then resumed at the repository user's explicit instruction after they had been informed of the rule and unresolved eligibility. That instruction is not organizer permission. We are preserving the files and their true chronology.

Could you please provide or obtain an authoritative written determination on the following?

1. Does this existing pre-event work affect our ability to participate locally or enter global judging? Which rules and decision authority apply?
2. What, if any, existing code, planning, processed data, figures or draft material may be used during the event? If this approach cannot be entered, what participation options are allowed? We do not assume deleting files, renaming the project, or restarting code automatically resolves the issue.
3. What exact activities, if any, may continue before the event, and under what scope? Our remaining preparation questions are: **A1**, text-only architecture/methodology planning; **A2**, access checks or downloading challenge DEMs without processing for connectivity resilience; **A3**, a separate generic application template without challenge logic. We understand that permission for one activity does not authorize others or establish eligibility for the existing prototype.

We also request the following event arrangements so that we can plan within the permitted schedule:

4. What is the local judging submission deadline? May a project page be edited after the local pitch before the global deadline?
5. What are the pitch duration, language, demo/slide requirements and available projection/internet facilities?
6. How must participants under 18 be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, and what documentation is needed? We will keep identifying images and voices of minors out of submission materials.
7. May teams work at home on the evening of November 14 or before the venue opens on November 15, within the official hackathon period?
8. May a team that has already selected its challenge work during the November 14 briefing/team-formation block?
9. What Wi-Fi, power outlets and extension leads are available, and may teams bring their own hotspot?
10. Is a prewritten role allocation and work schedule permitted, apart from any project code/materials? Please distinguish this organizational preparation from the existing technical documents disclosed above.
11. What are the local judging criteria and nomination process for global judging?

Please distinguish local participation conditions from a ruling applicable to global judging, and quote the exact scope and any conditions of an exception. We will record the written answer and disclose the true timeline; we are not requesting that early work be described as work produced during the hackathon.

Thank you,

Egiz team contact
